# Changelog

## Release candidate · 0.7.0-phase5a

### Survival progression

- Added crafting and processing recipes for the registered equipment, including tiered sheets, the Mana Mechanism, the Alfheim Mixer’s Terrasteel alloying, and Sylvan armor.
- Sylvan Mechanical Crafting keeps the source armor’s enchantments, trim, and custom name.
- Added Alfheim infusion categories for JEI and EMI, and wrench-toggle Manasteel sight glasses.

### Corporea and airships

- Added Mixer, Spout, Saw, and Mechanical Crafter support to Corporea autocrafting. The terminal shows active jobs and allows individual cancellation.
- Relay Sparks now link to Archive Pylons. Wireless terminal bindings follow their terminal through ship assembly and disassembly.
- Corporea pipe light flows toward the nearest wired terminal through bends and fittings.
- Mana Levitation Pylons hover at signal 0, ascend at 1–7, and descend at 8–15. Boot thrusters default to two midair bursts that reset on landing.

### Recovery and presentation

- Crafting cancellation and ship removal recover staged items. Mana Battery detonations release container contents, including aboard affected ships.
- Added Request Interface and Mana Levitation Pylon status screens, formed-multiblock goggles readouts, and a clearer Battery fill indicator.
- Added an Alfheim press attachment model, more sound subtitles, and a lower-right lance charge and cooldown indicator.
